Modeling notes

What the model assumes

  • Floor-to-floor set at 10′‑0″ — the sheets are plans only, with no section or heights.
  • Walls, openings and room shapes come from the drawing’s own vector geometry at 1/4″ = 1′‑0″.
  • Window heads at 7′‑0″, sills at 2′‑6″, doors at 6′‑10″. Not dimensioned on the sheets.
  • Both stairs are switchbacks: the run the first-floor sheet draws (11 treads at 10⅛″), a half-landing at the east end, then a return flight west. 17 risers at 71⁄16″ per level.
  • The office’s rear wall is modeled solid — the sketch does not detail glazing there.
  • Furniture, fixtures and cabinetry are omitted.

Three things worth a look

  • The stairwell opening as drawn leaves 5′‑11½″ of headroom at its west edge, about 8″ short of the 6′‑8″ minimum. Carrying the opening roughly 1′‑6″ further west clears it.
  • The return flight lands on 9¼″ treads, just under the 10″ minimum — the shaft is about a foot shorter than 17 risers want at this floor height.
  • The first-floor hallway wall is modeled stopping at the stairwell, which is how it would have to be built for the return flight to pass over it.

Checked against 17 of the dimension strings printed on the sheets — overall size, every labeled room, the garage door and the deck. Largest deviation: 1″.
